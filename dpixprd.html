warning The Problem: Screenshot Stitchers

The Guruprasad Story

At 10:42 AM, Guruprasad tapped 'Pay' on his mutual fund SIP. His bank instantly sent an SMS: ₹10,000 debited. But the investment app showed 'payment failed.'

Three systems. Three different answers. His money sat in limbo for days.

His bank said success. The payment gateway said failure. The mutual fund said failure. All were technically correct within their own systems.

error_outline This is not a bug. It is the architecture.

India's financial systems operate on fundamentally different clocks. OTPs expire in 60 seconds. UPI sessions timeout in 180 seconds. Mutual fund NAV allocation follows T+1 or T+2 rules. Each clock is rational for its purpose. No clock accounts for the others.

Why Push, Not Pull: The Technical Reality

Mobile operating systems (Android and iOS) increasingly restrict apps from reading other apps' notifications or SMS for privacy reasons. A "pull" model where DPIx scrapes signals from other apps is technically fragile and policy-constrained.

warning The Pull Model Problem

NotificationListenerService (Android) and UNUserNotificationCenter (iOS) require explicit user permissions and are subject to OS-level restrictions. Apps like banking apps actively prevent notification reading. This makes pull-based evidence gathering unreliable at scale.

The solution: Push-based evidence forwarding.

Push Model

forward_to_inbox DigiLocker as Transaction Inbox

Instead of DPIx pulling signals, each system pushes transaction evidence to a designated DigiLocker email address (e.g., user@digilocker.gov.in). DigiLocker evolves from static document storage into a dynamic transaction inbox.

Banks, payment gateways, and service providers already send transaction emails. Routing these to DigiLocker creates a trusted, verifiable evidence stream that DPIx can orchestrate.

Pull Model (Fragile)

• DPIx requests SMS permission

• DPIx requests notification access

• OS blocks sensitive app notifications

• Banks update apps, break scraping

• Unreliable, policy-constrained

Push Model (Robust)

• User sets DigiLocker as transaction inbox

• Banks email receipts to DigiLocker

• Payment gateways push confirmations

• DPIx reads from trusted DigiLocker API

• Reliable, government-backed, scalable

Strategic Evaluation: India Stack Integration

By anchoring the "Digital Dropbox" in DigiLocker (already the trusted national repository) and using Aadhaar/UPI purely as "Intent Rails," DPIx evolves from a standalone app into the Experience Layer (Layer 4) of India Stack.

This transforms DPIx from a "utility tool" into a Protocol for Journey Evidence.

AI Capabilities

1
Temporal Correlation

Matches timestamps across SMS, notifications, and app states to reconstruct what actually happened.

2
Status Translation

"Authorization failed" → "Bank ne confirmation maanga lekin samay par nahi mila. Aapka paisa safe hai."

3
Proactive Alerts

Detects anomalies (payment sent, no confirmation received) and alerts user before they discover the issue.

4
Evidence Assembly

Auto-bundles relevant signals into a chronological PDF with timestamps, ready for dispute resolution.
