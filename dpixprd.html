warning The Problem: Screenshot Stitchers

The Guruprasad Story

At 10:42 AM, Guruprasad tapped 'Pay' on his mutual fund SIP. His bank instantly sent an SMS: ₹10,000 debited. But the investment app showed 'payment failed.'

Three systems. Three different answers. His money sat in limbo for days.

His bank said success. The payment gateway said failure. The mutual fund said failure. All were technically correct within their own systems.

error_outline This is not a bug. It is the architecture.

India's financial systems operate on fundamentally different clocks. OTPs expire in 60 seconds. UPI sessions timeout in 180 seconds. Mutual fund NAV allocation follows T+1 or T+2 rules. Each clock is rational for its purpose. No clock accounts for the others.

layers DPI Positioning: The Three-Tier Ontology

The Centre for Digital Public Infrastructure (CDPI), co-chaired by Pramod Varma, notes that the foundational DPI categories are "not an exhaustive list". What is the gap?

lightbulb
Key Insight: India Stack handles flows of identity, money, and data. But no layer handles journey continuity across these flows. This is the "missing stitch."

The Emerging Ontology of DPIs

public Ecosystem Vision

DPIx is not just an app; it is the Agentic Glue that binds India Stack components into coherent user journeys.

The Vault

folder DigiLocker (Storage)

Role: Secure, government-backed vault for journey evidence bundles. Transaction receipts, warranty cards, consent records.

The Key

fingerprint Aadhaar (Identity)

Role: "Log in with Aadhaar" via App Intent. Zero-knowledge proof of identity. Face authentication without typing OTPs.

The Rails

payments UPI (Payments)

Role: Execution of value transfer. DPIx sets up the payment context; UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe) execute it.

The Brain

psychology DPIx (Experience)

Role: The User Agent. It calls the Key, rides the Rails, locks results in the Vault, and explains everything in the user's language.

translate "Kya Likha Hai?" The Query Interface

The farmer's wife in rural Bihar attempting her first SIP is not an edge case. She is the median user in a country where so-called edge cases are the majority.

Her natural question, "Kya likha hai?" (What does it say?), is not a limitation to be designed around. It is a design requirement to be designed for.

Design Principle: "Kya likha hai?" should be a feature, not a barrier.

AI Capabilities

1
Temporal Correlation

Matches timestamps across SMS, notifications, and app states to reconstruct what actually happened.

2
Status Translation

"Authorization failed" → "Bank ne confirmation maanga lekin samay par nahi mila. Aapka paisa safe hai."

3
Proactive Alerts

Detects anomalies (payment sent, no confirmation received) and alerts user before they discover the issue.

4
Evidence Assembly

Auto-bundles relevant signals into a chronological PDF with timestamps, ready for dispute resolution.
