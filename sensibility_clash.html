Historical Trajectory

From Mono to Plural Sensibilities

Historically, societies lived under a Mono-Sensibility—a dominant, singular aesthetic influence. Modernity, driven by industrialization and scientific acceleration, has shattered this into Plural-Sensibilities.

While our environment has exploded with diverse aesthetic stimuli, our biological hardware—the "Human Form Factor"—remains awkwardly unchanged. We are ancient biological entities navigating a world of exponential inputs.

Material Philosophy

Is AI the "Plastic" of Intelligence?

Think of natural materials: Stone, Wood, Iron. They have character and inherent limitations. Then think of Plastic—a "bastard material." It can be anything except itself. When it is itself, it is a shapeless, repulsive lump.

Does the software layer share this quality? Synthetic intelligence, like plastic, mimics everything but possesses no natural identity. It is performant yet spiritually hollow.
