01 / THE PROBLEM

Screenshot Stitchers

Behind every complaint is someone assembling screenshots, presenting them to institutions that already possess the data but will not correlate it.

The Guruprasad Story

At 10:42 AM, Guruprasad tapped 'Pay' on his mutual fund SIP. His bank instantly sent an SMS: ₹10,000 debited. But the investment app showed 'payment failed.'

Three systems. Three different answers. His money sat in limbo for days.

This is not a bug. It is the architecture. India's financial systems operate on fundamentally different clocks. OTPs expire in 60 seconds. UPI sessions timeout in 180 seconds. MF NAV allocation follows T+1 or T+2. No clock accounts for the others.

The Inverted Distribution
Where Bharat actually lives in heterogeneous populations
The Critical Inversion: Traditional fintech assumes a normal distribution where the middle class is the mass market. In India, the "tail" of excluded and underserved users is not an edge case—it's 1.36 billion people facing combinatorial barriers that create a million distinct journeys. The distribution isn't just skewed—it's inverted. Canonical UX can only serve the privileged minority; generative adaptation becomes structurally necessary.

04 / THE FRAMEWORK

Billion Users, Million Journeys

Rethinking UX means generating the PATH to the interface, not just customizing the interface itself.

LAYER 1

Canonical (During)

Fixed, auditable, regulatory compliance. SEBI regulations, KYC/AML, legal disclosures. Server-Driven UI (SDUI). Same for all users—the non-negotiable foundation.

LAYER 2

Generative (Journey)

AI-powered barrier detection and path generation. Analyzes context across 8 dimensions. Generates the PATH to the UI, not the UI itself.

LAYER 3

Rhetorical (Before)

GATO audio guidance, context inference, language selection, trust building, complexity reduction, personalized onboarding. Million rhetorical variations possible.

Key Insight: A billion users can have a million different journeys TO the form. But they all complete THE SAME FORM. The canonical layer remains deterministic; the rhetorical layer adapts.
