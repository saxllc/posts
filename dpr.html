Design Philosophy

From Power Laws to Platform Economics

An exploration of market concentration, unbundling, and the unique architecture of Digital Public Infrastructure in emerging economies.

① The Theoretical Foundation

Three layers that describe the same phenomenon at different levels of abstraction.

Mathematics

Power Laws

Zipf's Law & Pareto Distributions. A small percentage controls a large portion. Distribution = rank ∝ 1/frequency.

Economics

Increasing Returns

Brian Arthur's Framework. Advantages accumulate via lock-in and network effects. Small historical accidents lead to dominance.

Strategy

Unbundling & Rebundling

Sangeet Paul Choudhary. Systems cycle between fragmentation and concentration. Power sits with those who control the "rebundling point."

② The Missing Connection

Bridging business strategy with mathematical reality.

The Bridge

How they connect.

Unbundling creates fragmentation. Rebundling creates reconcentration. The result is a fat-tailed distribution.

③ Central Tensions

Trade-off

Efficiency vs. Concentration

Fragmented markets offer choice but high search costs. Concentrated markets offer ease but high supplier power.

Trade-off

Coordination vs. Extraction

Platforms create value by coordinating (playlists, logistics) but extract value by gatekeeping access to that coordination.

④ The Emerging Market Paradox

Where standard Silicon Valley theory breaks down.

Formal Sector

Like the West: Banks, Telcos. Winner-take-all dynamics.

Informal Sector

Millions of micro-enterprises. Chaotic fragmentation. Relationships over protocols.

DPI Layer

Standardized protocols (UPI, Aadhaar). Distributed power.

⚠️ The Critical Gap

Standard Theory Assumes:

A single unified market where price signals coordinate everything.

Reality:

Two parallel, minimally-connected markets (Dual Economy). Power fractures by layer rather than concentrating at a single point.

⑤ The Breakthrough: DPI

Case Study AADHAAR & UPI

Solving for scale without gatekeepers

The Problem: How to create efficiency in a fragmented dual economy without handing total control to a generic monopoly like Amazon?

The Solution: Unbundle the atomic unit (Identity, Payment) into a public protocol. This creates "Fat-tailed capabilities" (many providers) without "Fat-tailed power" (one boss).

⑦ Strategic Takeaways

For Strategists

Power doesn't accrue to aggregators in fragmented markets. It accrues to rebundlers who establish new control points.

For Designers

The atomic unit (file, ID, payment token) determines the system structure. Change the atom, shift the power.
